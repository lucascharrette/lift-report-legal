What Ski Monitor is

Ski Monitor reports which ski lifts are open or closed, along with weather and snow information, and can notify you when a lift you are watching changes status.

It is a convenience. It is not an official source.

Do not rely on it for safety

This matters more than anything else here, so it is near the top.

Ski Monitor is not a safety tool, and you must not use it to decide whether terrain is safe.

The app reports what public data sources say. Those sources can be wrong, delayed, or offline. A lift shown as spinning may be on hold. A lift shown as closed may have reopened. Information may be minutes or hours out of date, and the app will show you the last thing it saw when it cannot reach the server.

Avalanche conditions, closures, weather hazards and terrain status are decided by the resort and by ski patrol. Their signs, ropes, announcements and staff are authoritative. Ski Monitor is not. If the two disagree, the resort is right.

Always check with the resort before acting on anything the app tells you.
